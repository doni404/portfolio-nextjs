Asset type: Professional editorial cover for a technology journal, composed as a bold premium video thumbnail.

Create one original, polished, photorealistic editorial composite for the article "Dario Amodei's AI Slowdown Proposal: What Pacing the Frontier Means". The subject is the debate over the pace of frontier AI development, not a signed pause agreement or a complete halt to AI research.

Art direction: A confident, high-impact magazine cover with the visual clarity of a premium technology YouTube thumbnail. Favor a few large elements, tangible materials, dramatic but controlled studio lighting, and extremely legible typography. Make it feel designed by an experienced editorial art director, not a generic AI illustration.

Composition: Wide 16:9 landscape canvas. Reserve the left 48 percent for typography on a clean near-black charcoal background. Use the right 52 percent for a close-up, three-quarter-view brushed-metal AI compute module and an oversized tactile pace-control dial. Its pointer is turned away from the fast end toward a calmer middle setting; not fully off. A small bank of realistic server hardware sits behind it in crisp but lower-contrast detail. Use a few restrained horizontal light trails approaching the control and becoming ordered lines after it, suggesting speed meeting oversight. The hardware must remain distinct and readable at a small thumbnail size. No humanoid robot, holographic brain, floating sphere, generic neural-network wallpaper, or decorative glowing orbs.

Typography: Render the following text exactly once each. Top-left, small but readable uppercase eyebrow: "DARIO AMODEI". The main headline beneath it is two lines: "SLOW DOWN" in very large white extra-bold condensed sans-serif, then "AI?" in even larger bright acid-lime letters. Under the headline, a short quiet white uppercase subline: "PACING THE FRONTIER". The letters must be correctly spelled, flat-faced and sharply rendered, never distorted into 3D objects. Maintain strong hierarchy and generous breathing room. All text and the key control must be inside 7 percent outer safe margins; nothing can be clipped. Do not add any other readable text, labels, numerals, logos, watermarks, or UI badges.

Palette and finish: Near-black charcoal, white, acid lime, natural brushed silver, and a restrained warm coral accent in one hardware light. High local contrast, realistic fine metal texture, subtle reflections, and crisp clean silhouette separation. The type is the first read; the tactile control is the second. Avoid clutter, excessive fog, blur, lens flare, bokeh blobs, neon rainbow gradients, cartoon rendering, generic stock-art composition, and fake screenshots.

Accuracy constraints: This is a conceptual editorial image, not a photograph of an actual meeting, device, or product. Do not invent portraits, celebrity likenesses, quotations, signed documents, claims of an agreed shutdown, or statistics. Do not depict physical damage or catastrophe. The question mark is essential: the headline introduces a debate, not a completed industry-wide action.
